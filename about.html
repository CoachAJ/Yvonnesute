Understanding Microcurrent Biofeedback

Some microcurrent systems use biofeedback or closed-loop technology to measure changes in the skin's electrical response and adjust stimulation during a session. These readings can help a trained practitioner identify areas that may warrant closer attention.

Microcurrent delivers very low levels of electrical stimulation through the skin. Depending on the device, settings, and intended wellness application, it may be used to reduce pain, support comfort, mobility, swelling, circulation, ATP, relaxation, and the body's natural recovery processes.

Research has also explored how properly dosed microcurrent may influence cellular energy processes, including ATP production. ATP is the energy cells use to carry out many of the functions involved in maintenance and repair. These effects depend on the current level, waveform, treatment parameters, device, tissue condition, and individual response.

Yvonne uses microcurrent as part of a broader wellness approach—not as a replacement for appropriate medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment.
