All Avazzia Life devices must be powered by 2 high-quality 1.5V AA Alkaline batteries. Rechargeable batteries are not recommended as their lower voltage may negatively impact biofeedback performance and void warranty coverage.

Are there any contraindications for microcurrent?

Microcurrent devices should not be used by individuals with implanted electrical devices (such as pacemakers or defibrillators) or during pregnancy without practitioner approval. Contact Yvonne if you have questions regarding specific health conditions.
